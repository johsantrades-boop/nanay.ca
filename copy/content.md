### Kitchen — https://nanay.ca/collections/kitchen

Fresh off the wok, grill, and fire!

**Silogs:**  Sinangag (Garlic Fried Rice) + Itlog (Egg)

Silogs are the staple to Filipino breakfast. But better throughout the day. Very comforting, tastes nostalgic, and feels like home. We fry our signature garlic fried rice in a wok for added smokiness and aroma. Paired with your choice of grilled meats, fried fish, or roasted eggplant. Topped with an egg (PRO-TIP: ask us for crispy fried wok eggs), fresh green onions, and in-house made garlic bits.

**Spiced Vinegar**: Perfect dipping sauce for Silogs to bring out its brightness and complete the experience.

**Silog Feast:** Share and enjoy Silogs with 6 of your loved ones

**Inihaw (BBQ) Feasts:** Filipino authentic grilled meats/mushrooms served over our signature garlic fried rice. “The Skewers” are tender pork marinated in a sweet and savoury Filipino bbq sauce. “Chicken Inasal” is boneless chicken legs (thighs and drumsticks) marinated in soy, garlic, ginger, lemongrass grilled over charcoal. “Vegan Shrooms Skewers” are organic King Oyster mushrooms marinated in the authentic Filipino bbq used for the skewers.

**Toyomansi:** Soy sauce and calamansi dipping sauce for the Inihaw Feasts. Pour it all over your rice too!
